## Unclear rules

- [A-dow2] Number-only buttons vs generic stat-buff patterns: abilities[] leaves out 'a button that only changes numbers (a zoom)', but Granularity gives stat buffs generic patterns, and overclock-self is the stat-buff pattern. A timed damage boost on a key (Kraken Bolts) could go either way. The rule should say whether a timed buff on a key is kept.
- [A-dow2] Humanoid vs power-suit body plan: There is no tie-break. Power-suit says 'bigger than a soldier' yet 'at small size it is infantry', and the armour soldier rule mentions 'a power suit worn by one soldier (body size small)'. Power-armoured soldiers such as Space Marines can be read either way, and the choice changes flavour_affinity (Empire scores the power suit).
- [A-dow2] Three-source limit on wikis that split a unit across sub-pages: The DoW2 wiki splits each unit into tabs (stats, weapons, abilities, upgrades, strategy).
