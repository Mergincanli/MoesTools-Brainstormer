# Batch R4: tool problems the agents reported

Mustafa asked on 11 November 2026 for the tool problems that agents report to be listed apart from their hesitations, and for the list to be read before the merge pass. These are the 46 lines from the prep (15 prep agents, 5 editors and 1 judge). The fill adds its own when R4 goes on.

## In groups

- **Faults of the repository's own tools, all three fixed with tests** (pull request #8):
  - the page reader showed hidden comments of a wiki page's source as if they were text;
  - the copy-check and the page reader read the Steam news feed (patch notes) as raw data, where a line break or a bold mark inside a sentence could hide a copied run;
  - the page reader left pieces of tags among the lines of mwomercs.com pages.
- **Sites that refuse or fail:** web.archive.org (connections reset, in every game's prep); planetaryannihilation.com and the new Master Unit List site (HTTP 403); the old Master Unit List links (HTTP 404); some Halo Waypoint pages (HTTP 500); Steam's own pages of a post (no post text; the feed works); the MechWarrior Online store (drawn by a script); one PA Lobby page that is not served.
- **Limits of the session:** a few commands were refused by the permission check (a look at the network proxy's state, a plain request to one site); GitHub repositories outside the session's reach could not be listed (the game data was read as raw files instead); waiting inside a command is blocked.
- **Limits of the tools to know about:**
  - inside an agent's work folder the plant test found no browser, so agents could plant only on wiki pages (the session ran the browser tests itself);
  - the page reader prints a wiki category's text but not its list of member pages (an agent read the list through the wiki's API);
  - the copy-check ends "incomplete" on any folder, because 38 pages that older records cite cannot be fetched; this is known and listed in the handoff.
