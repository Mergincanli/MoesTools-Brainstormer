### Unclear rules (13)

- **Matchups rule 5 and firing-port carriers**: 'A unit that harms no enemy with a weapon or an ability' does not say whether a passenger passive whose riders shoot their own guns counts as the carrier harming. This decides whether the Battle Bus tooltip's 'strong vs. infantry, buildings' goes in counters or in combos. I put it in combos and kept counters to what each unit foils by itself.
- **roles.secondary step 5 for riders firing their own weapons**: The rule does not say which riders count as options (main faction only, the generals' own, captured or allied). It also does not say whether official strong-against text about an unarmed carrier can rank a role above step 5, or how to count riders whose port fire no source confirms (Terrorists).
- **on_death basis when the source shows an outcome no term covers**: The basis rule knows only the listed in-game kinds versus 'simply gone'. A special outcome like a second life has no slot. I used the 3C proposal with a fit_note.
- **Modes for one-way, death-triggered forms**: modes[].via must be 'the pattern that switches to this mode', but no pattern switches a form on death. I used passenger-sets-weapon because its wreck-bunker variant describes the Battle Bus.
- **modes[].target when the game's own unit kind differs from the sources**: The wiki calls the bunker a structure and gives it a building armour set, but the engine keeps it a vehicle kind. The rule does not say which wins.
- **Armour-class step 4 for improvised vehicles**: 'The kind of unit named in the class descriptions' is ambiguous for a reinforced civilian bus used as an APC (trucks are light, APCs medium). The registry's 'one-off sets map by what the unit is' does not settle it.
- **variant_of versus 'a unit of its own'**: The registry makes general's units with their own names units of their own, while variant_of is for 'general-specific versions'. It is unclear whether both apply to the Combat Chinook; I set variant_of.
- **tier.gate with a production building and a separate prerequisite**: The rule does not say which to name when both are on the tier map at the same band (Supply Center versus War Factory).
- **Ability kind for an ordered action with no button**: Supply gathering is ordered by sending the unit to a dock. 'Passive' is defined as always on, and the button-based kinds need a key.
- **Scope of Paired entries' 'never both'**: It is unclear whether passenger-sets-weapon and carry-passengers exclude each other only on the passenger passive or across all of a unit's abilities. This matters for a rope-drop button on a fire-port carrier (carry-passengers' hot-drop variant).
- **Interceptor armour model wording**: The meaning says 'few charges and must reload', but its own 'Seen in' is the Generals point-defence laser, which fires continuously with no clip.
- **Repeating a link in one matchup list**: No rule says whether the same ref may appear twice in strengths or weaknesses with different reasons. I did so for passenger-sets-weapon on the Battle Bus.
- **Repeated slot numbers in Generals command sets**: The registry does not say how to read a CommandSet that assigns one slot twice (the Stealth General's Battle Bus). I assumed the later line wins, but I did not verify that in the engine code.

### Unclear rules (8)

- **Scout through an upgrade's sensor (roles.secondary step 5)**: Step 5 lets a role meet its requires through the part that gives it. But an upgrade's detector must go in sensors.notes, and the validator reads requires only from sensors.traits, weapons and patterns. So any role that enters this way always shows as an atypical 'requires' break. The rule's only example is an anti-air option gun, which the validator can see. It is also unclear whether a detector add-on on a slow super-heavy tank should 'fit' scout's description at all. Ties at step 5 then go by roles-list order, which puts scout ahead of support and transport.
- **Tactics text as a role label (roles.secondary step 1)**: I could not tell whether a manual's general tactics advice counts as official text 'calling the unit' by a role alias. Here the Zero Hour manual lists Technicals for the GLA's probing and reconnaissance attack groups, under the heading Probing and Reconnaissance. The choice decides scout against raider for the Technical.
- **Off-screen for add-ons**: Range bands say an add-on is judged against the fog figure of the unit that carries it. A short-sighted carrier (the Overlord) therefore makes any long-reach add-on off-screen, including a Gattling gun, which is an odd result for a gun that is not artillery.
- **Damage step 4 with differing names**: Step 4 needs 'one game damage category'. It does not say what to do when two sources give the same weapon different map keys that map to the same type (Comanche Vulcan on the wiki, GATTLING in Weapon.ini). I treated it as one category, and the result is the same either way.
- **On-death outcome from upgrade-only seats**: The registry's garrison note codes the Overlord's Battle Bunker riders as an in-game bail-out. The fields doc does not say whether an outcome resting on passenger seats that only an add-on bought for that unit provides counts as the unit's own outcome. It only excludes powers or upgrades bought for the whole faction.
- **Size stand-ins that disagree**: Two kinds of the game's own size data disagree for the Overlord: its collision box is about twice the Battlemaster's, but it takes the same room in a transport. The sizes rule lists 'a size group in the code or rules' and 'the room it takes in a transport' together, without saying which wins. The examples also overlap: 'tanks of every weight' are large, 'tanks at least twice a battle tank's size' are huge.
- **Splash from a radius only in the 1.04 files**: The splash rule says a radius figure in the sources counts. The registry research note says terms that only the 1.04 files would set are not set. I followed the registry: the Technical's rockets get no splash (their radius is only in Weapon.ini), while the Overlord's cannons get splash, since the wiki infobox gives a radius.
- **accessed dates for cached pages**: Pages were read from the shared page cache. I used each page's cache fetch date as 'accessed' (2026-10-19 to 2026-10-27), not the day I read it. The fields doc says only 'date the page was read'.

- **Siege for a unit whose only stock attack is an innate button**: roles.primary step 1 counts innate buttons, but siege's coding rule and requires speak of weapons, and the validator reads requires only from weapons. For the Nakovalnya the validator is satisfied only through a veteran-only alternate weapon.
- **Alternate slot when no weapon is replaced**: The slot rule assumes the mode silences a weapon. For the Nakovalnya it silences only a button (the Bombardment ability).
- **Infobox weapon slots not marked (Ability)**: The registry note lists the marked ability slots, but the Nakovalnya's Bombardment sits in an unmarked slot and still behaves as an ability. The note could say which evidence wins.
- **Hybrid wheel-and-leg movers**: There is no class or mobility type for a mech that rolls on wheels pushed by legs. Also, both wheels and legs-multi leave out very-slow speed.
- **Mobility traits from veteran buttons**: deploy-anchor is kept for a button only veterans get, because mobility traits have no stock-only rule like the one for sensor traits. The opening check (Kaiser) did the same.
- **roles.secondary step 2 for buttons**: It is unclear whether a splash button supports anti-infantry, and whether a turret built from a menu can give anti-air (I applied the engineer-or-area-denial tie-break instead).
- **Official text naming support**: Change Log #04 remarks that players enjoy support roles, about medics' and engineers' healing and repairing. Whether that is official text calling the unit by the role (step 1) or only describes play style decides the order [support, anti-armour] vs [anti-armour, support].
- **Shared faction scope**: The registry's 'shared' faction is described as the common aircraft and weapon systems only. The Ids convention and the registry's kit-squad note would put the engineers there too, yet Usonia's bunker line differs. The merge pass should confirm.
- **Build menus: pattern per item and one source per entry**: The registry's game-wide note names only build-cover and lay-mines for the engineers' fortifications. Unclear: does a garrisonable bunker serve build-cover or carry-passengers, and does an armed bunker serve deploy-turret? Also, a pattern entry that mixes items every engineer has with veteran-only items can carry only one source term.
- **Context orders vs buttons**: Repair and barbed-wire cutting may be right-click orders rather than buttons. The abilities rule counts buttons.
- **Unit links to unresearched shared weapon systems**: The ids for the heavy machine gun and mortar ('heavy-machine-gun'? 'field-mortar'?) are not fixed. I linked only iron-harvest.shared.field-cannon, as the reconciled Kaiser entry did.
